Common Questions

How do I sign in?

GotchuReviews uses Google Sign-In. Tap "Sign in with Google" and use your Google account to get started.

How do I scan an invoice?

Tap the Scan tab, then use your camera to capture an invoice or select one from your photo library. The app will automatically extract vendor and pricing details.

How do review credits work?

You earn credits by submitting reviews with verified invoices. Credits can be used to view detailed reviews and pricing information from other users.

How do I delete my account?

To request account deletion, please email support@gotchureviews.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

Report a Bug

Found a bug? Please email us at support@gotchureviews.com with a description of the issue and steps to reproduce it.
