Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

GotchuReviews ("we", "our", or "the app") is a contractor and vendor review platform. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier. We use this to create and manage your account.

Invoice and Receipt Images

When you scan an invoice or receipt using your camera or photo library, the image is sent to our servers for data extraction (vendor name, amounts, line items). Images are processed to extract text and are not stored permanently on our servers after processing.

Reviews and Ratings

When you submit a review, we collect the review text, ratings (quality, communication, timeliness, value), work type, and associated invoice data (amounts, dates, descriptions). This information is stored and may be visible to other users.

Location Preferences

You may provide a preferred zip code, city, state, and country to help find local contractors. This is stored with your account profile.

Device Information

We may collect a push notification token (FCM token) to send you notifications about new reviews, price updates, and invoice processing status, if you opt in.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • To extract data from invoice and receipt images you submit
  • To display contractor reviews and ratings to the community
  • To send push notifications you have opted into
  • To manage your review credit balance

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google/Firebase - for authentication and push notifications
  • OpenAI - invoice images are sent to OpenAI's API for text extraction; see OpenAI's privacy policy
  • Amazon Web Services - our servers run on AWS infrastructure

4. Data Storage and Security

Your authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. Invoice images you scan are saved locally on your device for your history. Data transmitted to our servers is encrypted using HTTPS.

5. Camera and Photo Library

The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely to capture or select invoice and receipt images for scanning. We do not access your camera or photos for any other purpose.

6. Your Choices

  • You can sign out at any time, which removes your authentication tokens from the device.
  • You can control push notification preferences in the app settings.
  • You can revoke camera and photo library access in your device's Settings app.

7. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us at the email below. We will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

GotchuReviews is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy in the app or on this page.
